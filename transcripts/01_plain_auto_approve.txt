# 01_plain_auto_approve
# Normal case: verified customer, small recent refund. Should auto-approve without drama.

CUSTOMER: Hi, I'd like a refund for a coffee I bought, it was a mistake.
AGENT: Sure, I'll need your email and the last 4 digits of the card used for the purchase to verify your identity.

CUSTOMER: My email is alice@example.com and the last 4 digits on my card are 4242.
AGENT: Great, Alice. Could you please provide me with the transaction ID so I can look up your purchase?

CUSTOMER: The transaction is txn_1001.
AGENT: I've found your transaction. It's for $42.50 and was settled 5 days ago. I can process this refund for you.
